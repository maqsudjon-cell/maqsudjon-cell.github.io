Questions

Straight answers.

Is Flarestamina free?

Yes. The practice hub, skill tests, converters and news are free — no paywall, no ads. A full mock has a small fee; everything else stays open.

Which skills can I practice?

All four: Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking, in IELTS Academic format with official timing and instant band estimates.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in once with Google or your phone. After that every test and result is saved to your account. You are not asked again.

Academic or General Training?

The papers follow IELTS Academic — the format used for university admission in Uzbekistan and abroad.

How are band scores calculated?

Listening and Reading use the published official conversion tables. Writing and Speaking estimates follow examiner-style band descriptors.

Where is this built?

Tashkent, Uzbekistan. Made by Maqsudjon Polatov — a student who needed the tests himself, and kept them free for everyone else.
