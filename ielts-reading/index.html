Questions students ask

Are these tests free?

Yes — every test here is free and needs no sign-up.

How long is the IELTS Academic Reading paper?

Three passages, 40 questions, 60 minutes — about 20 minutes per passage.

Will I get a band score?

The full 40-question tests convert your raw score to an IELTS band. A single passage shows a raw score instead, because a band is only meaningful over all 40 questions.

Can I use the highlighter and notes like in the real exam?

Yes. Select any text in the passage to highlight it or attach a note, exactly as you can in the exam.
